GOOD TO KNOW

Questions,
answered.

Still deciding if DAUUZ is for you? Start with these common questions.

Ready to join? ↗
Is DAUUZ free to join?+

Yes. Creating an account and participating is free. No payment is required.

How do I earn DUZ?+

Complete available activities such as sharing on X, referring friends, maintaining wellness routines, answering surveys, sending feedback or submitting ideas. Each task has its own reward details.

Why are the rewards small?+

DAUUZ currently states a fixed total supply of 30,000,000 DUZ. Its reward model is designed around distributing contributions sustainably over time.

Can I sell my DUZ now?+

No. DUZ is currently tracked off-chain and is not tradeable.

When will DUZ move to the blockchain?+

Blockchain deployment is a future plan. The current focus is building and testing the participation system first; no on-chain launch is available today.

How are submissions checked?+

DAUUZ describes a review process that can include submitted evidence, manual checks and account-level fraud prevention. Submitting a task does not guarantee approval.

Can I complete tasks more than once?+

Limits depend on the activity: X posts are limited to four per day; wellness routines are once daily; feedback is once weekly; surveys are once total. Referrals and ideas may be submitted more than once, subject to the task rules and review.

What is Zoi?+

Zoi was described in earlier DAUUZ materials as a planned wellness companion concept. Check current project announcements for the latest status before relying on this integration.

Who is behind DAUUZ?+

DAUUZ describes itself as a project being built in public, with its development and progress shared openly.

What if I lose access to my account?+

Use the password reset option on the login page. If you still cannot access your account, contact the project team through its official support channel.
